## Who this is for

The Value Forge covers one beat: **the older person's journey through acute and community care** — acute frailty, rehabilitation and therapies, palliative and end-of-life care, and care delivered out of hospital, with clinical psychology read across all of them. Five clinical domains, one population, one question: where does NHS practice stand against the strongest available evidence, and where is the gap?

It is written for the people who hold that journey's design in their hands. **System and community leaders** — ICB and provider — get the cross-domain pattern: where converging evidence points at a structural constraint, and where policy and evidence are moving in different directions. **Clinicians** in these specialties get a weekly, sourced read of what the literature actually showed, at the epistemic weight it actually carries. **Anyone accountable for value** gets every finding filtered through one prior standard: does this shift a patient's remaining time toward function and away from acute dependency?

## What it is

The Value Forge is a systematic clinical intelligence archive. It gathers, assesses, and synthesises published evidence across five domains weekly, and produces a monthly meta-synthesis — the flagship Monthly edition, a deep cross-domain review substantially fuller than the weekly synthesis.

The site is a presentation layer over an already-running pipeline. The pipeline produces finished, dated intelligence. The website makes it permanent, navigable, and genuinely public.

## The archive/gallery distinction

The organising principle is the distinction between an **archive** and a **gallery**.

An archive is complete, chronological, honest about provenance. Nothing disappears. Everything is dated, sourced, versioned. The history is the point. The archive earns trust through its discipline: you can see not just what the system currently believes, but what it believed before, and what changed it.

A gallery is curated, sequenced, argued. It makes a claim about what matters right now. Its authority depends on the quality of the curation, not the completeness of the record.

The Value Forge is built on both. The archive — every output, every finding, every pattern, dated and versioned — is the foundation. The gallery — the editorial front page, the Monthly, the curated signal summary — is the argument made from it. The credibility of the gallery depends on the discipline of the archive.

## Why it exists

Three live justifications:

**Archive integrity.** A publication pipeline without a permanent home compresses navigable history. Git-as-archive solves this structurally: every finding is dated, versioned, and non-revisable without a visible trace.

**Discipline.** A public archive imposes honest dating regardless of readership. The constraint holds with zero readers. Making intelligence genuinely public — not institutional output dressed as transparency — is itself an epistemic commitment.

**Consilience horizon.** The substrate for an eventual evidence contribution. The systematic record is the precondition.

Public reach is demoted. It is an aspiration, not a present justification.

## What it cannot claim

The Value Forge closes the epistemic gap and the translation gap. It does not close the incentive gap or the capacity gap. That distinction is named here because honest scope is what makes the intelligence trustworthy to people who have learned to distrust institutional claims.
