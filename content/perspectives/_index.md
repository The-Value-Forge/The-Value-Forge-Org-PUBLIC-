---
title: "Perspectives"
description: "Featured original articles and invited contributions — named, argued, and distinct from the publication's synthesised intelligence."
---

Perspectives carries featured original articles and invited contributions: longer, argued pieces under a named byline. They sit apart from the synthesised Signals, Syntheses and Monthly review — a Perspective is an author's argument, not a pipeline output, and is presented as such. Contributions are held to the same static-copy discipline: any factual or clinical claim carries a verified source.
