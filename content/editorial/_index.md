---
title: "Editorial"
description: "Signed, first-person reflections from the editor — distinct from the publication's synthesised clinical intelligence."
---

Editorials are signed, personal reflections in the editor's own voice. Where the Signals, Syntheses and Monthly review speak institutionally and pass through the publication's evidence-fidelity discipline, an editorial is a first-person perspective — and is marked as such, so the reader always knows which voice they are reading.
