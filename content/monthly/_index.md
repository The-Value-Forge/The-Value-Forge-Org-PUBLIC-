The Monthly is The Value Forge's flagship publication — the deep cross-domain review. It synthesises evidence, patterns, and findings across all five domains into a structured deep dive, published every month and substantially more detailed than the weekly synthesis.

Each edition includes: editorial; pattern recognition; domain-by-domain review (D01–D05); absence detection; cross-domain synthesis; an international benchmark of NHS practice against world best practice; a synthesising observation; and a horizon scan.
