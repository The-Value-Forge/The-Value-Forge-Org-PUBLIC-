## What this site is — and is not

The Value Forge is an independent clinical intelligence publication. It synthesises published evidence from NHS practice, policy, and international health systems for a professional audience, under a published production method and constraint system.

It is **not** an NHS service or NHS publication. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, funded by, or produced on behalf of the National Health Service, NHS England, or any NHS trust, integrated care board, or other health body. References to "the NHS" describe the health service as the publication's national subject, not any relationship with it. No NHS branding is used, and none may be inferred.

## Not medical or professional advice

Nothing on this site is medical advice, and nothing here creates a clinician–patient relationship.

The content is editorial synthesis of published research and policy, written for a professional readership. It is not a substitute for the judgement of a qualified clinician with the patient in front of them, and it must not be used to make decisions about the diagnosis, treatment, or care of any individual. **Never disregard, or delay seeking, professional medical advice because of something read here.** If you need medical help, contact your GP or NHS&nbsp;111; in an emergency, call&nbsp;999.

The same limit applies professionally: nothing here constitutes commissioning, procurement, financial, or legal advice. Evidence summarised on this site carries explicit labels — `Provisional` and `Contested` findings are labelled precisely because they are **not** a settled basis for decisions. The labels are part of the content; a reader who ignores them is not reading the publication it publishes.

## Accuracy, currency, and the limits of the promise

This publication is produced under a [published constraint system](/method/production/) designed to keep every claim tied to a verifiable source, and it maintains a public [corrections process](/corrections/). That is a discipline, not a guarantee. Evidence changes; studies are revised, superseded, and retracted; errors are possible despite the system built to prevent them.

Content therefore speaks **as of its stated date** and is provided **"as is" and "as available"**, without warranty of any kind, express or implied — including any warranty of accuracy, completeness, currency, or fitness for a particular purpose. Where content is found to be wrong, the remedy this publication offers is the one it promises: a dated, public correction.

## Automated production

The Value Forge is produced substantially by automated large-language-model routines operating under a versioned constraint contract, with a human editor accountable for the whole. This is disclosed in full, including what the automation may never do, on [How this publication is produced](/method/production/). That page also carries the publication's independence and conflict-of-interest statement, including its complete separation from the editor's employment.
