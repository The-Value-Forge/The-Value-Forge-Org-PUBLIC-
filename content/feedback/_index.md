The Value Forge is in alpha. This page turns the publication's own ethos on itself: **name the gap, state honest scope, close the loop.** Tell us what's wrong, unclear, or missing — and below, in the open, see what we did about each thing.

We publish **themes and our responses**, never your identity or any identifying detail. If you leave an email it stays private and is used only to follow up with you.
