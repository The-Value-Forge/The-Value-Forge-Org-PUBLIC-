A meta-analysis examined the Clinical Frailty Scale (CFS) as a prognostic tool specifically in elderly patients with heart failure [Meta-analysis] (Sebastian & Yehya, DOI: 10.1016/j.cpcardiol.2026.103432, PMID: 42641835).

The finding is condition-specific: it establishes CFS's prognostic value within a cardiology-frailty population, and should not be read as evidence that CFS performs equivalently across all frailty pathways, or as a general endorsement of CFS over other frailty-assessment tools outside heart failure.

For services managing the interface between cardiology and frailty pathways, the finding supports using CFS as a prognostic marker specifically in elderly heart-failure caseloads.
