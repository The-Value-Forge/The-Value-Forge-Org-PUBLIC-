# Hero headline — editorial pass options (priority 6)

_5 Jul 2026, drafted under the `article-writing` skill during the autonomous stretch.
Mocked on the real hero (screenshots sent to the editor); **nothing shipped** — this is
the editor's pick. The em-italic accent word is part of each option._

| # | Headline | What it's doing |
|---|---|---|
| **Current** | Clinical intelligence, *forged* from evidence. | The brand pun. Safe, dignified, a little abstract — says the category, not the position. |
| **A** | What good looks like — and the *gap* made visible. | The mission sentence, verbatim. The strongest claim to "point of view"; instantly says what the publication does that others don't. |
| **B** | Where NHS practice *stands* against the world's best. | The one question the five domains share. Reads like a standing dashboard; frames every page below it as evidence toward the answer. |
| **C** | Evidence, weighed by the *time* it gives back. | The VAT lens made explicit — time as the measure of value. Most distinctive; also the most oblique for a first-time visitor. |
| **D** | Five domains. One question: what does *good* look like? | The synthesis-as-USP framing. The only question-form option; energetic, but a question headline cedes a little authority. |

**Editorial note (Claude's view, not a decision):** A is the truest to the charter —
it is the mission doing the work, and "the gap made visible" earns the page's
authority rather than asserting it. B is the strongest conventional alternative.
C is worth keeping for the About/Method page even if not chosen here — it is the
best one-line statement of the VAT lens we have.

Standfirst unchanged in all options. Whichever is chosen, ship it with the
static-copy + decoupling checks re-run on the landing page (no new factual claims
are introduced by any option, so the check is a formality).
